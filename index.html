Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 June 2026  ·  Applies to all RightBonds products and browser extensions including Amazon Past Purchase History (APPH)

Short version: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who we are

RightBonds develops browser extensions and web tools. Our products are designed to run entirely on your device without sending data to any external server.

2. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, process, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information
  • Browsing history or activity
  • Purchase history or order data
  • Financial information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or cookies

3. Amazon Past Purchase History extension

The APPH Chrome extension works entirely within your browser:

  • Your Amazon Order History CSV is parsed locally in your browser using JavaScript.
  • Parsed data is stored in Chrome's local storage on your device only — it never leaves your browser.
  • The extension makes no network requests to any server.
  • No analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party services are included.
  • Clearing extension data via the popup permanently deletes all stored data from your device.

4. Third-party libraries

The extension uses PapaParse, an open-source CSV parsing library. It runs entirely in your browser and sends no data anywhere.

5. Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • storage — to save your parsed order data locally on your device.
  • host permissions for Amazon domains — to read product page information (ASIN, price) so the extension can display your past purchase history. No page data is transmitted externally.

6. Children's privacy

Our products are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of our products after changes constitutes acceptance.
