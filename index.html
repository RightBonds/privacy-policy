Introduction

We understand this is a big responsibility.

RightBonds develops browser extensions and web tools designed to run entirely on your device, without sending data to any external server. The short answer to "what do you do with my data?" is simple: nothing leaves your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no data for us to gather.

The extension makes no network requests, so none of the following is ever collected, stored on our side, processed, or transmitted:

  • Personal identification information
  • Browsing history or activity
  • Purchase history or order data
  • Financial information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or cookies

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to lose, sell, or hand over.

How the extension works

Three steps, all of them on your device.

Parsed in your browser

Your Amazon Order History CSV is read locally with JavaScript, using the open-source PapaParse library. The file is never uploaded anywhere.

Stored only on your device

Parsed data is saved in Chrome's local storage on your machine — and nowhere else. It stays there across sessions until you remove it.

No servers, no tracking

The extension makes zero network requests. There are no analytics, no tracking scripts, no cookies, and no third-party services.

Permissions we request

Only what's needed to show your prices — nothing more.

storage

To save your parsed order data locally, on your device.

host · amazon domains

To read product page information (price and ASIN) so the extension can match and display your past purchases.

These permissions are used only to read and display. No page data is ever transmitted externally.

Sharing your information

We don't — because we never have it.

Since the extension keeps everything on your device, there is no information for us to share with companies, partners, advertisers, or anyone else. No data leaves your browser, so none can be passed on.

Keeping it secure

The safest data is the data that's never sent.

Our security model is the architecture itself: by never transmitting or storing your data on a server, we remove the most common ways information is exposed. Your parsed data lives only in your browser's local storage, on your own device, under your control.

Deleting your data

One step, and it's gone for good.

You can remove everything the extension has stored at any time. Clearing extension data from the popup permanently deletes all stored data from your device — there are no server copies, because none exist.

Clear dataOpen the extension popup and choose "Clear data."

Children's privacy

Not directed at children under 13.

Our products are not intended for children under 13, and we never knowingly collect information from children. Since the extension collects no information from anyone, this holds true by design.

About this policy

When it applies, and how it changes.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Amazon Past Price History extension. The extension uses PapaParse (5.5.3), an open-source CSV parsing library that runs entirely in your browser and sends no data anywhere.

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of our products after changes constitutes acceptance.

Introduction

We only look where you ask us to — and only on your device.

Bookmark Suggestions identifies your most-visited domains from the past 7 days of Chrome browsing history and places them as favicon bookmarks in your bookmarks bar. The extension reads your local history, writes bookmarks, and stores your preferences — all on your device. No data is transmitted to any server.

What we collect

We read locally, store locally, and share nothing.

The extension accesses the following data solely on your device:

  • Browsing history (7-day window) — domain names and visit counts, read from Chrome's local history API to rank your top sites. This data never leaves your browser.
  • Preferences — your chosen number of suggestions, hidden sites list, and toggle state, stored in Chrome's local storage.
  • Bookmark IDs — IDs of the bookmarks created in your bar, stored locally so the extension can update them on refresh.

None of the following is ever collected:

  • Page content or URLs beyond the domain
  • Personal identification information
  • Financial or location data
  • Cookies, device identifiers, or analytics

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to lose, sell, or hand over.

How the extension works

Four steps, all of them on your device.

History read locally

Once per day at 12:01 AM (your local time), Chrome's history API is queried for the past 7 days. Only domain names and visit counts are used. The raw URLs and page titles are processed in memory and discarded immediately.

Bookmarks created in your bar

The top sites (up to your chosen number) are added as favicon bookmarks to your Chrome bookmarks bar using the Chrome Bookmarks API — a local browser operation. Old bookmarks from the previous refresh are removed first.

Preferences stored locally

Your settings (number of suggestions, hidden domains, enabled/disabled state) are stored in Chrome's local storage on your device only. They are cleared automatically when you uninstall the extension.

No network requests, ever

The extension makes zero outbound network requests. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no third-party services of any kind.

Permissions we request

Four permissions — each with a single, narrow purpose.

history

To read domain names and visit counts from your local 7-day browsing history. Used only to rank and suggest your most-visited sites.

bookmarks

To create and remove favicon bookmarks in your Chrome bookmarks bar. Used only to display and update your suggestion set.

storage

To persist your preferences (number of suggestions, hidden sites, enabled state) and the current bookmark set across browser sessions.

alarms

To schedule a daily refresh at 12:01 AM local time so your suggestions stay current without requiring the browser to be restarted.

No permission is used for any purpose beyond what is described above. No data accessed via these permissions is transmitted externally.

Sharing your information

We don't — because we never have it.

The extension processes everything locally. We have no server, no database, and no mechanism to receive your data. There is nothing to share with companies, partners, advertisers, or anyone else.

Keeping it secure

The safest data is the data that's never sent.

Our security model is the architecture itself. By never transmitting your browsing history or preferences to a server, we remove the most common vectors for data exposure. Your data lives only in Chrome's local storage, on your own device, under your control.

Deleting your data

Two ways to clear everything.

You can remove all stored data at any time:

Remove All (in popup)Toggle "Remove All" in the popup to clear bookmarks from the bar before uninstalling.
UninstallChrome automatically clears all local storage when the extension is uninstalled. No data persists.

Children's privacy

Not directed at children under 13.

Our products are not intended for children under 13, and we never knowingly collect information from children. Since the extension collects no information from anyone and transmits nothing, this holds true by design.
